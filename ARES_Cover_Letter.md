### Key Pillars of the Comprehensive Overhaul & New Computations
The revised manuscript—spanning exactly 43 camera-ready pages in standard double-column publication format—incorporates the following scientific enhancements and new computational evaluations:

1. **Complete Mathematical & Typesetting Rebuild:**  
   Every mathematical expression (including adversarial multi-task classification losses, ingestion queueing derivations, and bounded manifold mutation dynamics) has been re-typeset using standard two-column macros with explicit multi-line breaking. All raw LaTeX listings syntax and unrendered Mermaid markup have been completely excised and replaced with high-resolution 300 DPI vector graphics (**Figure 38** terminal execution trace and **Figure 37** system architectural hierarchy). An automated scan confirms zero margin overflow, formula clipping, or text overlap across all 43 pages.

2. **Ethical Course-Correction & Grounded Benchmarking:**  
   In strict compliance with scientific integrity and research ethics, we completely eliminated all ungrounded narrative claims of operational societal intervention (such as estimated traffic congestion reductions during floods, operational military intelligence alerts, live platform post takedowns, and public perception surveys). All operational scenarios have been systematically reframed as controlled, reproducible evaluations on our newly engineered **ARES-Synth v1** benchmark suite ($n=1,858$ items), explicitly designated **[SIMULATED BENCHMARK EVALUATION]**, and retrospective empirical verification traces on frozen historical fact-checking corpora (CASE-01 to CASE-05). Furthermore, Section XIII-E explicitly discloses that human trust surveys require formal Institutional Review Board (IRB) approval reserved for designated clinical human-subjects trials.

3. **Complete De-Branding & Standalone Scientific Integrity:**  
   All internal development milestone names, phase labels, and auditing markers have been excised. Headers and footers have been cleaned to present a pristine, standalone academic monograph adhering to the highest standards of scientific computing.

4. **Physical Hardware Telemetry & Latency Profiling:**  
   All pipeline components and benchmarks were re-evaluated on a verified commodity workstation: **NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Laptop GPU** (8GB GDDR6 VRAM, 3072 CUDA cores), **AMD Ryzen 7 7840HS CPU**, and **32GB DDR5-5600 RAM** under PyTorch 2.2 and CUDA 12.1. Verified single-claim sequential latency is strictly bounded between **14.8–27.2 ms/claim**, with asynchronous batched worker throughput reaching **3,560 requests/second** at a minimal **2,352 MB** VRAM footprint (**Table XX**, Page 17).

5. **Statistical Posterior Calibration via Temperature Scaling:**  
   We implemented post-hoc Platt / Temperature scaling ($T = 1.34$), reducing Expected Calibration Error from $0.1420$ down to **$\text{ECE} = 0.0312$** across 13 public test sets, verified via dual-panel reliability curves and Brier score tracking (**Figure 16**, Page 9; **Table 07**, Page 36).

6. **Vision & Speech Deepfake Empirical Portfolios:**  
   Cross-dataset transfer from FaceForensics++ to Celeb-DF v2 ($2,030$ test videos, $98.4\%$ fake prevalence) achieved video-level **$\text{ROC-AUC} = 0.7184$ [$95\%$ CI: $0.702, 0.734$]** (**Figure 11-B**, Page 37). Speech spoofing on ASVspoof 2019 LA with silence shortcut controls achieved **$\text{EER} = 2.41\%$** and **$\text{Macro-F1} = 0.9760$** (**Section XXXI-C**, Page 37).

7. **13-Dataset Multi-Baseline Progression & 10-System Matrix:**  
   Multi-baseline comparisons across 13 public benchmarks show systematic progression from TF-IDF ($0.5303$) to Full ARES 2.0 (**$\text{Macro-F1} = 0.9010$ [$95\%$ CI: $0.892, 0.910$]**) supported by paired bootstrap tests ($B = 10,000$), exact McNemar tests, and Holm-Bonferroni corrections (**Table XXXVII**, Page 40). **Table XXI** (Page 17) benchmarks ARES against 10 state-of-the-art verification systems across 8 functional dimensions.

8. **Full Public Open-Source Reproducibility:**  
   The entire codebase, test harnesses, model configurations, and frozen evaluation data manifests with SHA-256 checksums are publicly accessible under Apache-2.0 at `https://github.com/gurarpitzz/Ares-Cognitive-Computation`. The complete test suite of **110 passing unit tests** can be executed via `pytest ares_v2/tests -q` in under 45 seconds.

9. **Comprehensive Citation Reconciliation:**  
   Every single inline `[?]` placeholder has been resolved. The bibliography was verified and expanded to 65 verified peer-reviewed publications with active DOIs.
