## 1. Description of Blocked Research Activities & Ethical Grounds

In the original manuscript, Section XIII presented several narrative case studies asserting dramatic real-world deployment impacts:
1. **Assam Flood Disaster Traffic (Claim C024):** The authors claimed an operational ARES deployment issued a Level-3 alert in 18 minutes that directly reduced panic traffic congestion by 47% and was integrated into a national disaster authority dashboard.
2. **Ladakh Border Military Escalation (Claim C025):** The authors claimed an operational ARES system transmitted classified intelligence alerts to military command in Ladakh within 12 minutes, preventing an international military escalation.
3. **Social Media Post Moderation (Claim C027):** The authors claimed that ARES autonomously triggered or mediated the removal of 13,000+ bot-amplified posts across Twitter and Facebook.
4. **Public Perception Survey (Claim C030):** The authors claimed an empirical survey of 4,000 respondents demonstrated a +62% trust increase for ARES relative to traditional fact-checking services.

### Scientific Integrity & Legal Analysis:
- **Zero Fabrication Policy:** Under strict scientific integrity protocols, empirical claims regarding external world events, institutional actions, or human psychological reactions must be grounded in primary verifiable records (e.g., official platform API audit logs, government agency memoranda, or signed participant consent manifests).
- **Institutional Ethics (IRB) Mandate:** Conducting human perceptual surveys ($n=4,000$) requires formal Institutional Review Board (IRB) or Human Research Ethics Committee approval, pre-registered survey instruments, documented sample demographics, and data protection safeguards. No IRB approval number, survey instrument, or raw questionnaire responses exist.
- **National Security & Defense Boundaries:** A public academic research repository cannot claim operational classified military alerting without formal institutional authorization, defense clearance manifests, and classified record provenance. Claiming classified military intervention in an unclassified academic paper is unacceptable.
- **Platform Moderation Boundaries:** Platform post takedowns require direct coordination with platform trust & safety teams (X/Meta) and cryptographically signed moderation audit logs. No platform partnership agreements or takedown IDs exist.

## 3. Retrospective Case Study Alternative

In place of fabricated real-world impact metrics, the revised manuscript presents **empirical retrospective verification traces** evaluated on real historical claims drawn from public fact-checking databases (Claim C026, Table `table_retrospective_traces.csv`). These traces document:
- Real input text and source metadata.
- Empirical retrieval passages from frozen Wikipedia/AVeriTeC corpora.
- Calibrated posterior probabilities $P(Y=\text{false} \mid x)$.
- End-to-end processing latencies measured on the declared hardware platform.

All such analyses are explicitly qualified as **retrospective offline verifications**, strictly avoiding any claim of real-time deployment impact or causal influence on external events.
