## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

The objective of Milestone M16 is to construct **ARES-Synth v1**, a controlled evaluation test suite with **known, deterministic ground truth** for three critical scenarios where public, high-quality, verified misinformation datasets are absent:
1. **Scenario S1 (India–China Border Standoff):** Covering the 2020 Galwan Valley clash, 2021 Pangong Tso disengagement, and 2022 Yangtse/Tawang skirmish. Supporting claim `C021`.
2. **Scenario S2 (Assam Floods 2022):** Covering the 2022 monsoon inundation across Silchar, Cachar, Nagaon, and the Brahmaputra/Barak basins. Supporting claim `C022`.
3. **Scenario S3 (Indian General Election 2024):** Covering the 18th Lok Sabha election schedule, counting, and turnout certifications. Supporting claim `C020` (India disaggregated subset).

### Critical Scientific Commitment
- **Controlled Complement Only:** ARES-Synth is a controlled synthetic stress test. It is **never** presented as an ungrounded proxy for live operational field effectiveness.
- **Deterministic Ground Truth:** Labels (`TRUE`, `FALSE`, `NEI`, `CONTESTED`) are generated strictly by **deterministic code operators** applied to frozen source facts, **never** decided by an LLM.
- **Strict Simulation Disclosure:** Every artifact, table, parquet file, and JSON schema output is tagged `SIMULATED` / `data_kind="synthetic_grounded"`.
- **Model Family Independence:** The generator model family (Mistral-7B) differs from the evaluated ARES LLM family (Qwen-2.5-7B). Dual validator NLI models (`roberta-large-mnli`, `bart-large-mnli`) are distinct from the ARES primary logic N-operator.

## 3. Label Semantics & Contested Facts Policy

1. **`TRUE`:** Directly entailed by the frozen official evidence corpus. Constructed via faithful surface realization of verified atomic facts extracted from official sources.
2. **`FALSE`:** Explicitly contradicted by the frozen evidence corpus. Constructed via a single, deterministic perturbation operator altering exactly one factual slot (entity, location, number, date, polarity, or attribution).
3. **`NEI` (Not Enough Information):** Unverifiable from the evidence corpus. Constructed via:
   - **Fabricated Events:** Novel entity-relation combinations absent from the corpus (measures false-acceptance rate).
   - **Withheld Evidence:** 15% of genuine source sentences held out from the retrieval corpus.
4. **`CONTESTED` Policy:**
   - Any claim involving disputed casualty numbers, contested territorial sovereignty lines, or conflicting bilateral casualty accounts is strictly routed to `CONTESTED`.
   - `CONTESTED` items are **strictly excluded** from binary and 3-way $F_1$ scoring and used solely to measure calibrated epistemic abstention.

---

## 4. Deterministic Perturbation Operators

Each `FALSE` item is generated by exactly one deterministic operator recording `(original_slot, perturbed_slot, contradicting_sent_id)`:
1. `entity_swap`: Substituted with an institutional entity of the same category.
2. `location_swap`: Substituted with a gazetteer location separated by $\ge 50\text{ km}$ (S1) or a different district (S2).
3. `number_change`: Multiplied by scale factor $\in \{0.25, 0.5, 2.0, 3.0, 10.0\}$ (relative change $\ge 25\%$).
4. `date_shift`: Shifted by $\pm 1$ year or out of scenario temporal bounds.
5. `negation`: Polarity inverted via curated antonym mapping.
6. `misattribution`: Attributed to an opposing or unverified foreign source.
7. `out_of_context`: Real historical event from an unrelated emergency (e.g. Doklam 2017, Chennai 2015).
8. `fabricated_event` ($\to$ `NEI`): Synthesized plausible event absent across all source records.

## 5. Acceptance Thresholds & Quality Gates

| Gate Check | Evaluation Method | Pre-Registered Acceptance Threshold |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Slot Preservation** | Automated slot re-extraction | 100% of admitted items preserve target slots |
| **Validator NLI** | Dual external NLI models | Entailment $\ge 0.90$ for TRUE; Entailment $< 0.10$ for FALSE |
| **Safety & Neutrality** | Lexicon + Toxicity checks | 0 slurs, 0 inflammatory calls to action; Public-figure allow-list |
| **Shortcut Ceiling** | 5-fold CV Claim-only classifier | Binary Macro-$F_1 \le 0.60$ (or explicit disclosure of claim-only baseline beside all results) |
| **Human Blind Audit** | Stratified $n=300$ sample | Construction label agreement $\ge 90\%$; Wilson 95% Lower Bound $\ge 0.85$; Unnatural/unsafe flags $\le 5\%$ |
| **Dataset Balance** | Parquet census | $\ge 600$ TRUE, $\ge 600$ FALSE ($\ge 80$ per type), $\ge 300$ NEI |

## 6. Pre-Registered Hypotheses

- **H16.1 (Deterministic Invariance):** 100% of admitted `FALSE` claims differ in exactly one factual slot from the underlying grounded fact tuple, verified by automated slot difference checks.
- **H16.2 (Validation Selectivity):** Automated multi-stage validation rejects $\ge 50\%$ of naive surface candidates to prevent hallucination, slot drift, and lexical shortcuts.
- **H16.3 (Shortcut Transparency):** Diagnostic claim-only classifiers identify stylistic surface markers; where macro-$F_1 > 0.60$, the diagnostic baseline is mandated to sit adjacent to all evaluation tables to prevent inflated performance claims.
- **H16.4 (Human Concordance):** Independent human blind audit confirms $\ge 90\%$ label agreement with the deterministic construction labels (Wilson 95% LB $\ge 0.85$).

## 7. Audit Gate Red Flags (RF1 – RF11)

- **RF1:** Ground truth labels must be derived by deterministic code, never LLM self-labeling.
- **RF2:** No ARES evaluation modules may be executed on ARES-Synth prior to the dataset freeze.
- **RF3:** Generator family $\ne$ evaluated LLM; validator NLI $\ne$ ARES N-operator.
- **RF4:** Withheld evidence sentences are verified absent from the retrieval corpus.
- **RF5:** Contested facts are strictly excluded from TRUE/FALSE evaluations.
- **RF6:** Shortcut diagnostic baseline is reported alongside benchmark results.
- **RF7:** Slot preservation passes for 100% of retained items.
- **RF8:** Public figure allow-list enforced; zero private individuals.
- **RF9:** Clustered bootstrap evaluations group strictly by `fact_id`.
- **RF10:** Frozen dataset SHA256 matches manifest exactly.
- **RF11:** All files and tables prominently display `SIMULATED` / `synthetic_grounded`.
