## 2. Processor (CPU)

* **Model:** 13th Gen Intel® Core™ i7-13620H
* **Architecture:** Raptor Lake-H (Intel 7 process)
* **Core Count:** 10 Physical Cores
  * **Performance Cores (P-cores):** 6 Cores, 12 Threads (Base: 2.40 GHz, Turbo: up to 4.90 GHz)
  * **Efficient Cores (E-cores):** 4 Cores, 4 Threads (Base: 1.80 GHz, Turbo: up to 3.60 GHz)
* **Total Threads:** 16 Threads
* **Cache:** 24 MB Intel® Smart Cache
* **Thermal Design Power (TDP):** 45 W Base (up to 115 W Maximum Turbo Power)
